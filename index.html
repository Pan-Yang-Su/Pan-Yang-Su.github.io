Research

Species distribution modeling under climate change

Current focus, 2023–present

I develop hierarchical Bayesian models to predict how range shifts respond to warming, using long-term monitoring data from alpine and boreal ecosystems. Recent work extends this to short-term forecasting for conservation planning.

Open tools for ecological forecasting

Software & reproducibility

I maintain open-source R and Python packages that make it easier for field ecologists to fit and validate distribution models without a statistics background.
